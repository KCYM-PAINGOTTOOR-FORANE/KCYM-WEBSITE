About KCYM

The Kerala Catholic Youth Movement (KCYM) is the official state and regional organization under the KCBC. It is a vibrant community of young people dedicated to spiritual growth, social action, and community building.

Founded on the principles of Christ's teachings, we strive to be the salt of the earth and the light of the world, engaging in activities that promote justice, peace, and love.

KCYM Paingottoor

Our Forane, Paingottoor, is a vibrant community of young people united in faith, fellowship, and purpose. As part of the KCYM family, we come together to grow spiritually, lead with conviction, serve our community, and create a meaningful impact through our initiatives. Carrying forward the spirit and vision of KCYM, we strive to be a generation rooted in faith and rising for change.
